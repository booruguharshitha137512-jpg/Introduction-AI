What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems designed to simulate human intelligence. These systems perform tasks such as visual perception, decision-making, language translation, and problem-solving.

What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a subfield of AI focused on building algorithms that learn from patterns in data. Instead of being explicitly programmed, ML models improve their accuracy over time through experience.

Where is AI Used?

AI powers everyday technologies like voice assistants (Siri, Alexa), recommendation algorithms (Netflix, Spotify), autonomous vehicles, medical diagnostic tools, and smart web search platforms.
